# Notes

Short concept notes written as you learn. Over a year these become your own textbook, and the best ones become posts.

## Where notes go

| Kind of note | Where |
|---|---|
| Rough, quick, written on your phone or between meetings | [Scrape Codebook on GitBook](https://anirudh-docs.gitbook.io/scrape-codebook), in an **AI Infrastructure** section |
| Cleaned-up concept notes, with numbers and diagrams | Here, one Markdown file per concept |
| Polished explanations for others | A post on your [profile site's blog](https://anirudh6415.github.io/blog/) |

**Flow:** rough note in GitBook → clean it up here when the week's topic is done → turn the best ones into posts.

To add a note: copy the [template](template.md) to `content/notes/<topic>.md` and fill it in. It appears in the sidebar automatically.
